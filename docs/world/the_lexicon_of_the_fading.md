# The Lexicon of the Fading

There are words we no longer speak. They belong to a world that believed in sharp edges and finalities.

We do not speak of *wrong* or *evil*. These are judgments for those who have the luxury of distance. We speak of *burdens* and *weights*.
We do not speak of *sin* or *guilt*. We speak of *echoes* and *remnants*.
We do not speak of *punishment* or *deserving*. We speak of *ends* and *stillness*.

The language of the world is now the language of the hearth and the grave. It is the sound of cooling metal and the scent of ozone. It is the silence that follows a long-held breath.
