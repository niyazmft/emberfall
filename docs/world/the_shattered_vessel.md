# The Shattered Vessel

In the time before the Great Fading, there were many who called themselves Keepers. They did not carry burdens; they carried light. But as the metal grew cold and the ozone thick, the light flickered and died.

Now, only the Shattered Vessel remains. It is not a thing of glass or clay, but a memory of a duty once held dear. To be the Vessel is to hold what others have dropped. To carry the echoes of paths that ended too soon.

It is a mourning-duty. There is no judgment in the carrying, only the heavy, silent acknowledgement of what was lost. The metal groans in sympathy, and the embers glow with the heat of lives stilled.
